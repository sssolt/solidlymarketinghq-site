About SolidlyLeads

A performance marketing partner for roofing companies that want qualified homeowner opportunities, not vague marketing activity.

Our Mission

SolidlyLeads helps roofing companies create a more controlled way to buy leads. Roofers should not have to choose between expensive retainers and shared lead marketplaces that send the same homeowner to every competitor in town.

We help define what a qualified roofing lead means before delivery starts: territory, roof issue, homeowner details, timing, and contact quality. The goal is a lead flow your team can judge by booked inspections and real sales conversations.

Who We Work With

SolidlyLeads is operated by Venith Emmerce LLC. For this roofing program, we work with roofing and exterior companies that want to buy opportunities for roof replacement, storm damage inspections, repairs, and related exterior projects.

We are not a generic directory or a bulk-data reseller. Our work sits closer to performance marketing: define the buying criteria, launch the acquisition system, validate lead quality, and use feedback to improve the flow over time.
